System controls

To enable Restart/Shut down, opt in on the Pi by adding PI_REMOTE_ALLOW_POWER=1 to ~/.config/pi-remote/runtime.env and restarting the Pi Remote service. Your Pi account must also have systemd power permissions.

Connection

The pairing is saved on this phone and never expires automatically. Don't clear Chrome's site storage.

Only use Pi Remote on your trusted network. Port 3100 must not be forwarded to the internet.
